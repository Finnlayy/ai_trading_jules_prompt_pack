6 Execution Steps
  • ① Regime Check — market regime before expensive AI
  • ② AI Review — swarm scouts render decisions
  • ③ Risk Engine — deterministic gates (confluence, spread, RR, cooldown)
  • ④ Broker Execution — simulation / paper / pionex_direct / glint / ctrader
  • ⑤ Live Fill Tracking — intent + fill + position monitoring
  • ⑥ Journaling — NDJSON + SQLite dual write
Monitoring & Exits
  • PricePoller — 10s ticker polling, PnL updates
  • PositionMonitor — stop-loss, take-profit, time-exit logic
  • Portfolio Circuit Breaker — hard kill on drawdown
